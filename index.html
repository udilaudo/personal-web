About Me

I'm a Data Scientist and Theoretical Physicist based in Trieste, Italy. My work sits at the intersection of physics, mathematics, and machine learning.

I recently completed my PhD at the University of Trieste, where I developed machine learning models for automated seabed morphology recognition. My research spans deep learning, computer vision, Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), and GIS-based spatial analysis.

Before that, I studied theoretical physics in Bologna, specializing in quantum phase transitions and deconfined quantum criticality.

Research

ODE Decoupling via Normalizing Flows

Combines Neural ODEs and Normalizing Flows to learn an invertible coordinate transformation that maps a coupled ODE system to a latent space where the equations become approximately decoupled. Each latent dimension is then governed by an independent neural network.

Research

Latent-Space Physics-Informed Neural Network

PhD collaboration with Prof. Anselmi (University of Trieste). Jointly trains a convolutional autoencoder and a PINN entirely in the compressed latent space to solve the inverse problem for 2D PDEs — discovering unknown operator coefficients from simulation data without finite-difference approximations.
