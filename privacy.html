Cookie Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how this personal portfolio website handles information when you visit it. This site is designed with privacy as a first principle: no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no personal data collected directly by the site owner.

Please read this policy alongside the Cookie Policy, which specifically covers cookie and local storage usage.

01.

Data Controller

The data controller for this website is:

Umberto Di Laudo
Trieste, Italy
Email: umbertodilaudo@gmail.com

As this is a personal portfolio with no commercial activity and no direct data collection, no formal Data Protection Officer (DPO) is required under Art. 37 GDPR.

02.

Data We Collect

This website does not collect, store, or process any personal data directly. There are no registration forms, no login systems, no contact forms that submit data to a server, and no analytics tracking.

The data that may be processed by third parties in connection with your visit is:

Infrastructure data (Cloudflare): When you request any page, your IP address, browser user-agent, request URL, and timestamp are processed by Cloudflare, the hosting and CDN provider. See Section 4 for full details.

Font loading data (Google Fonts): When a page loads, your browser makes a request to Google's font servers (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com) to download the typefaces used by this site. This request includes your IP address, browser user-agent, and the referring URL. See Section 7 for full details.

Theme preference (your device only): If you toggle the light/dark mode, your preference is stored in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device. See Section 5.

03.

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), data processing requires a lawful basis under Article 6. The relevant bases for this site are:

Legitimate interest — Cloudflare (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): The infrastructure data processed by Cloudflare is strictly necessary to technically deliver the website and protect it from abuse (DDoS, bot traffic). This would not be possible without handling basic connection data.

Legitimate interest — Google Fonts (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Loading fonts from Google Fonts is necessary to render the website with the intended typography. The site owner has a legitimate interest in presenting the site in a visually correct form, and this interest is not overridden by the visitor's rights given the minimal, non-profiling nature of the data involved. Google does not use font-loading connection data for advertising profiling.

No consent-based processing (Art. 6(1)(a)) is required because this site does not use analytics, advertising, or marketing trackers.

04.

Cloudflare Pages (Hosting Provider)

This website is hosted and delivered via Cloudflare Pages, a static site hosting service operated by Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA).

When you visit this site, your browser connects to Cloudflare's global edge network, which may process the following data as part of normal HTTP/S request handling:

IP address · browser user-agent string · HTTP request headers · URL requested · timestamp · HTTP response codes

For hosting-related processing, Cloudflare acts as a data processor on behalf of the site owner, under a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) compliant with GDPR. For its own network security and abuse-prevention operations, Cloudflare may act as an independent data controller. Data transfers to the United States rely on appropriate safeguards pursuant to Art. 46 GDPR, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (DPF), as applicable. Cloudflare does not use this data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

For complete details, refer to Cloudflare's own privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy .

05.

localStorage (Theme Preference)

When you toggle the light or dark colour theme, the site saves your preference as the string "light" or "dark" in your browser's localStorage under the key "theme".

This data never leaves your device. It is stored locally in your browser and is only read by this page to restore your chosen theme on future visits. No server receives this information. The stored value is not used by the site owner to identify users and remains entirely on the user's device.

You can clear this preference at any time via your browser's developer tools (Application → Local Storage → delete the theme key) or by clearing your browser's site data.

06.

Cookies

The site owner does not set any cookies. No analytics cookies, advertising cookies, session cookies, or tracking pixels are placed by this website.

Cloudflare, as the infrastructure provider, may set strictly necessary technical cookies (e.g. __cf_bm for bot management, valid for 30 minutes) as part of its security and load-balancing operations. These cookies are essential for the technical delivery of the site and do not track users across websites. No consent banner is required for strictly necessary cookies under the ePrivacy Directive.

For a full breakdown of all cookie-related information, including browser opt-out instructions, please see the Cookie Policy.

07.

Google Fonts & Libraries

This site loads the typefaces Inter and Playfair Display from Google Fonts, a free font hosting service operated by Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA).

When your browser loads any page on this site, it makes an HTTP request to fonts.googleapis.com (to fetch the CSS stylesheet) and to fonts.gstatic.com (to download the font files). These requests send to Google:

IP address · browser user-agent string · referrer URL · timestamp of the request

According to Google's own statements, Google Fonts does not use font-loading data to build advertising profiles, does not set cookies via the Fonts API, and stores font-request logs for a limited period for security and abuse-prevention purposes. Data may be transferred to the United States under Google's standard contractual clauses (SCCs) pursuant to Art. 46 GDPR.

The legal basis for this transfer is legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — the font loading is necessary for the correct visual rendering of the website. For full details, refer to Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and Google Fonts FAQ: developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy .

The icon library (Lucide Icons) is loaded from jsDelivr, a free open-source CDN operated by Prospect One sp. z o.o. (Warsaw, Poland — EU). When a page loads, your browser requests the script file from cdn.jsdelivr.net, which sends your IP address and browser user-agent to jsDelivr's servers. jsDelivr does not use this data for advertising and does not set tracking cookies. The legal basis is legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See jsDelivr's privacy policy: jsdelivr.com/privacy-policy .
