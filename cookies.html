Privacy Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which cookies (if any) are used on this website, and how you can manage your preferences. It should be read together with the Privacy Policy, which also covers data processed by Google Fonts and Cloudflare.

This website is a static personal portfolio with no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The cookie footprint is minimal and limited to strictly necessary infrastructure functionality. Note that Google Fonts and jsDelivr CDN (used for typography and icons) do not set cookies, but do receive connection data — covered in Privacy Policy § 7.

01.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit it. They are stored in your browser and sent back to the originating website on subsequent visits.

Cookies are typically used to:

Keep sessions active (e.g. remember you are logged in) · Store preferences (e.g. language, theme) · Analyse usage (e.g. page views, click paths) · Deliver personalised advertising

This website uses cookies only for the first category (infrastructure security), and no cookies for analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking.

02.

Cookies Set by This Website

The owner of this website (Umberto Di Laudo) sets zero (0) cookies. No first-party cookies of any category are placed on your device by this site's code.

Specifically, the following cookie categories are not used:

✗  Analytics / statistics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics, Matomo)
✗  Advertising / targeting cookies
✗  Social media tracking pixels
✗  Session or login cookies (no user accounts exist)
✗  Personalisation cookies

Because no consent-requiring cookies are set, no cookie consent banner is displayed — this is legally correct under the ePrivacy Directive and GDPR.

03.

Cloudflare Infrastructure Cookies

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and delivered via Cloudflare's global CDN. Cloudflare may automatically set strictly necessary cookies as part of its network security, bot management, and load-balancing operations. These are third-party cookies set by Cloudflare's infrastructure, not by the site owner.

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration Set By
__cf_bm Strictly Necessary Bot management and protection. Distinguishes between legitimate user traffic and automated bots to protect the site from abuse. 30 minutes (session) Cloudflare, Inc.
__cflb Strictly Necessary Load balancing. Ensures that requests from a given session are consistently routed to the same Cloudflare edge server. Session (deleted when browser closes) Cloudflare, Inc.
cf_clearance Strictly Necessary Set after a visitor has successfully passed a Cloudflare security challenge (e.g. CAPTCHA). Prevents the challenge from being shown again. Up to 30 minutes Cloudflare, Inc.

Note: Not all of these cookies will necessarily be set on every visit. Cloudflare only sets security cookies when its automated systems determine they are required (e.g. suspicious traffic patterns). On a normal visit from a residential connection, you may see none of these cookies.

These cookies are classified as strictly necessary under the ePrivacy Directive because they are required for the secure technical operation of the CDN infrastructure. No consent is required to set them. For Cloudflare's full cookie documentation see: Cloudflare cookie documentation .

04.

localStorage (Theme Preference)

This site uses the browser's localStorage API — not a cookie — to remember your light/dark mode preference. localStorage data is stored locally in your browser and is never transmitted to any server.

Key: theme  ·  Values: "light" or "dark"  ·  Scope: your device only, never sent to a server  ·  Consent required: No

localStorage entries do not expire automatically. To delete this preference: open your browser's developer tools → Application (or Storage) tab → Local Storage → select this site → delete the theme key. Alternatively, clear your browser's site data for this domain.

05.

Why There Is No Cookie Banner

Under the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC, as amended) and GDPR, a cookie consent banner is required only when a website sets non-essential cookies — i.e. cookies that are not strictly necessary for the technical operation of the service requested by the user.

This website sets no non-essential cookies. The only cookies that may be present are strictly necessary infrastructure cookies set by Cloudflare (Section 3 above). No consent banner is legally required or displayed.

This position is consistent with guidance from the Italian DPA (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali), the EDPB (European Data Protection Board), and the Article 29 Working Party Opinion 04/2012 on cookie consent exemptions.

06.

Managing and Deleting Cookies

You can control, disable, or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect the technical delivery of pages. Since this site sets no non-essential cookies, disabling cookies will not prevent you from accessing any content.

Instructions for managing cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Opera

You can also opt out of Cloudflare's bot protection cookies by contacting Cloudflare's privacy team at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy . However, blocking Cloudflare's infrastructure cookies may result in degraded performance or security challenges when accessing Cloudflare-hosted sites.

07.

If new non-essential cookies were ever introduced (for example, if analytics were added in the future), this policy and the Privacy Policy would be updated before implementation, and a cookie consent mechanism would be added as required by law.
